E-Commerce Return & Refund Policy
===================================

Last updated: November 2024

RETURN WINDOW
Customers may return items within 7 days of the delivery date. After 7 days,
returns are no longer accepted except in cases of manufacturing defects, which
are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by our support team.

ELIGIBLE ITEMS
Most items sold on our platform are eligible for return and refund, including:
- Electronics (must be in original packaging with all accessories)
- Clothing and footwear (must be unworn and with tags attached)
- Home appliances (must be undamaged and in original packaging)
- Books and stationery

NON-ELIGIBLE ITEMS
The following items cannot be returned or refunded:
- Perishable goods (food items, fresh produce)
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Personal hygiene items (once opened)
- Customized or made-to-order products
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

REFUND PROCESS
For eligible returns:
1. Contact our support team within the 7-day return window
2. Provide your order ID and reason for return
3. Our team will verify eligibility based on these policy rules
4. Approved refunds are credited within 3-5 business days

REFUND AMOUNTS
- Full refund: Available for defective items or incorrect deliveries
- Partial refund: Available if item shows minor wear; amount determined by support team
- No refund: Items returned outside the return window or in ineligible categories

HIGH-VALUE REFUNDS
Any refund request above Rs. 5,000 must be reviewed and approved by a human support
agent. These requests are automatically escalated and you will be contacted within
24 hours.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
If you received a damaged or defective item, please report it within 48 hours of
delivery. Include photographs of the damage when raising a support ticket. These
cases are given high priority.

ELECTRONICS RETURN POLICY
Electronics can be returned within 7 days if:
- The item is defective or not as described
- All original packaging, cables, and accessories are included
- The item has not been physically damaged by the customer

CLOTHING AND FOOTWEAR
Clothing items must be:
- Unworn (trying on is acceptable)
- Free of perfume, stains, or pet hair
- Returned with original tags still attached

SHIPPING COSTS FOR RETURNS
- If we made an error (wrong item, defective product): We cover return shipping
- If customer changed their mind: Customer covers return shipping

CONTACT SUPPORT
For refund and return queries, use our in-app support chat or raise a ticket.
Support hours: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST.
